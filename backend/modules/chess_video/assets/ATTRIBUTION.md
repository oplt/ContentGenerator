# Chess piece assets

PNG piece glyphs under `pieces/` were generated from Unicode chess symbols
rendered with **Noto Sans Symbols 2** (SIL Open Font License 1.1).

White and black pieces both use the filled (black-side) Unicode glyphs,
recolored: solid opaque white or near-black fill plus a contrasting outline.
(The white-side Unicode codepoints are outline-only and look translucent on
light squares.)

Font source: https://fonts.google.com/noto/specimen/Noto+Sans+Symbols+2

No runtime font or network download is required; the renderer loads these
local PNGs only.
